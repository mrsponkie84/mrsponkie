Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

TL;DR: I respect your privacy. I collect minimal data, never sell your information, and try to be transparent about everything. If you have questions, just ask!

👋 Hello!

I'm mrsponkie, the indie developer behind the apps and games you see on this site. This Privacy Policy explains how I collect, use, and protect your information when you use my website and apps. I believe in keeping things simple and honest, so let's dive in.

📊 What Information I Collect

Depending on how you interact with my services, I may collect:

  • Contact Information: Your name and email address when you use the contact form or sign up for updates.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous analytics about how you use my apps (like which features are popular) to help me improve them.
  • Device Information: Basic device info (like device type and operating system) to ensure my apps work properly on your device.
  • Game Progress: If you use cloud save features, your game progress and preferences.

🎯 How I Use Your Information

I use the information I collect to:

  • Respond to your messages and provide support
  • Improve my apps and fix bugs
  • Send you updates about new features or apps (only if you've opted in)
  • Analyze usage patterns to make better design decisions
  • Comply with legal obligations

🔒 How I Protect Your Information

I take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. This includes using HTTPS encryption, secure servers, and limiting access to personal data. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, so I can't guarantee absolute security.

🍪 Cookies and Tracking

My website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your experience. These help me understand how visitors use the site and remember your preferences. You can control cookies through your browser settings, but some features may not work properly if you disable them.

🤝 Third-Party Services

My apps and website may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Analytics: To understand app usage (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase)
  • App Stores: Apple App Store and Google Play Store for distribution
  • Payment Processors: For handling in-app purchases securely
  • Cloud Services: For data storage and app functionality

I recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these services for more information.

👶 Children's Privacy

My apps are generally designed for all ages. I don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the applicable age in your jurisdiction) without parental consent. If you believe I've collected such information, please contact me, and I'll promptly delete it.

🌍 International Users

If you're accessing my services from outside my country of residence, please note that your information may be transferred to and processed in different countries. By using my services, you consent to this transfer.

📝 Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information I hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt-out of marketing communications
  • Data portability

To exercise any of these rights, please contact me.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

I may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When I do, I'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. For significant changes, I'll try to notify you through my apps or website. I encourage you to review this policy periodically.

📬 Contact Me

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or my data practices, feel free to reach out:

  • Email: hello@mrsponkie.com
  • Contact Form: mrsponkie.com/contact
